Scope and exact claim
---------------------
The new literal binary table is obtained from the accepted pure-NF174 table
by the same two incoming-edge changes used in the NF+AxCount183-to-182
reduction. No AxCount formula or counting axiom is introduced. The pure-NF
source program remains byte-identical, including all 2,976 source addresses.

The old transitions d:163/read1 and d:79/read1 both write1, move right,
and enter d:39. Change the first successor to d:17 and the second to d:160.
No surviving row then enters d:39. Delete its two rows. Every other row is
unchanged. The resulting machine has 173 working states, 346 transition

This proof compares the two machines at complete source operations. The
deleted control and its replacements are not asserted equal on arbitrary
tapes or at every old primitive boundary.

The donor is distinct from every destination; external interior ingress

2. The physical contracts at each rotated loop
------------------------------------------------
The canonical frame is the established guard, source address, gap, header,
nine unary fields in order R2,R8,R1,R4,R3,R7,R0,R5,R6, and spare zero
fields. A register r has r+2 ones. Base gap is at least49, and there are
at least24 spare zero fields. The nine register extensions, gap extension,
and spare extension are independent unbounded natural exponents. A return
to control d:0 is accepted only with the full matching tape, source address,
head position and resources; internal visits to d:0 are not boundaries.

Let w be1 for the last loop's terminal wrap and0 otherwise. The following
five exact phase contracts have been generated for each literal head:

  Entry zero h: R3=0,R4=m -> exit with both unchanged;
    gap increases w, spares unchanged, frame origin decreases w.

  Entry positive h: R3=n>0,R4=0 -> h+1 with R3=n-1,R4=0;
    gap increases1, spares and origin unchanged.

  Defer h+1: R3=d,R4=0 -> h+2 with every register/resource unchanged.

  Tail positive h+2: R3=d>0,R4=m -> h+2 with R3=d-1,R4=m+1;
    gap increases1, spares increase1, origin unchanged.

  Tail zero h+2: R3=0,R4=m -> exit with R3=0,R4=m+1;
    gap increases w, spares increase1, origin decreases w.

All five preserve R1>0 and R7=0. All unnamed registers are independently
arbitrary and preserved. The tail contracts allow arbitrary destination m;
only entry-positive and defer require zero destination.

These are physical universal proof obligations, not tests at finitely many
register values. The trace producer emits explicit bit transitions and
whole-word pumps. Separate replay reconstructs its own domains/endpoints
and checks each transition and one-unit pump law. Pump iteration is then
natural induction. The physical Lean-kernel replay is a separate receipt.

3. Complete rotated-loop arithmetic and termination
----------------------------------------------------
If the original donor n=0, the entry-zero contract exits immediately and
performs no increment. If n=k+1, entry-positive and defer reach the tail
with donor k, destination0, gap G+1 and spares S.

For each i with 0<=i<=k the tail invariant is

  R3=k-i, R4=i, gap=G+1+i, spares=S+i.

Each positive tail decreases the donor by1, increases destination/gap/
spares by1, and retains all guards. After k such tails, the zero tail adds
the one remaining destination increment and exits. The complete output is

  R3=0, R4=n, gap=G+n+w, spares=S+n, origin=old_origin-w.

Those are exactly the old loop's complete effects on every admitted input.
The n=0 and n>0 cases cover all naturals. Each tail has finite physical
clock and strictly decreases the positive donor; hence no new infinite
internal execution or early halt is introduced. Resources never decrease.

Sixteen freshly regenerated natural-induction proof terms establish the
standard/rotated output identity, remaining donor, operation counts, and
gap/spare expressions. They have been accepted by the standalone Lean
kernel. Their unchanging generic export is bound afresh to all29 literal
NF loop occurrences and144 exact phase/source formulas by check_source.py.

5. Coverage, startup and whole-program composition
---------------------------------------------------
The physical domain inventory contains

  3,791 ordinary domains outside the replaced intervals,
    140 rotated-loop domains (28 times5), and
      1 exceptional borrowed-jump domain,
  for 3,932 universal domains in total.

Every ordinary decrement outside the replaced intervals has its positive
and zero domains. The original halt domain is included. The independent
phase-interface audit checks every domain, exact endpoint, head shift,
resource delta, and the unreachability of the discarded2915 phase.

Exact blank replay reaches PC0 after67,998 transitions with R1=1, all other
registers0, head-4, gap55 and30 spare fields. Every marked tape cell is
compared against the canonical frame. This satisfies the universal frame
and source-invariant premises above.

At every outer source boundary, induction combines an unchanged ordinary
primitive, an unaffected finite closed loop, a verified rotated loop, or
the verified borrowed loop. The inductive source invariant supplies each
entry guard. The complete register/resource effects match the unchanged
source; the next outer boundary again satisfies that invariant. Universal
phase correctness and donor decrease show that each such source operation
is implemented in finite time and cannot halt elsewhere.

Consequently the173-state machine halts from blank exactly when the
unchanged pure-NF source halts, and therefore exactly when the174-state
parent halts. Inheriting the parent's NF calibration gives the same
halting-if-and-only-if-NF-inconsistent claim with the parent's existing
qualification. This saving needs no AxCount axiom: only the shared machine
architecture, ordinary natural-number arithmetic, and proved source guards.

The whole-program composition in this document is a written proof supported
by machine-checked physical phases, source invariants, exact startup and
kernel-checked arithmetic. It is not a claim of one new end-to-end Lean
theorem from the NF axioms to binary halting, and it does not upgrade any
inherited written numerical-enumeration or model-calibration bridge.
