only d:163/read1 from d:39 to d:17, and d:79/read1 from d:39 to d:160.
All writes and moves remain the same. The now unused d:39 rows are removed.

reconstruction identifies 694 disjoint closed arithmetic loops, and checks

Kernel replay and proof boundary
--------------------------------
Raw physical proof terms quantify over all eleven natural parameters and
arbitrary exterior tape rays beyond explicit blank margins. They use only
the supplied equality/natural-number prelude, definitions, computation and
natural induction; there are no added axiom declarations. Every theorem is
regenerated from the bound table, source and traces before checking.
The complete physical replay receipt is kernel/phases.json when present.

The mathematical composition uses finite termination of each ordinary phase
and donor-decreasing induction for the rotated tails. It yields identical
source-boundary effects, including gap/spare growth. The identical source
and same initialized source counter then preserve the baseline's halting
behavior. The arithmetic induction is supplied by the separate transfer
workstream. No single end-to-end Lean theorem for the entire source/TM/NF
calibration is asserted here.

In particular, the inherited 174-state NF calibration still includes written
standard-natural numerical enumeration and zero-row census arguments. Its
abstract pure-NF calculus theorem is distinct from the literal implementation
bridge. This new reduction imports no Axiom of Counting and changes no logical
row or numerical proof-enumeration source instruction.
