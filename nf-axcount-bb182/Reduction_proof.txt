1. The literal change

The 183-state table has SHA-256
bb6603f5a5e9848c3125ce1c861ff5f6bbdb3db383bdbc65ad487fba1feb0b52.

Retain every transition except these two targets:

    state   read   write   move   old target   new target
    d:163     1      1      R       d:39         d:17
    d:79      1      1      R       d:39         d:160

The state d:39 then has no outside predecessor. Delete its two rows.
The resulting table has 182 working states, 364 binary rows, and a separate
halting state. Its start state is k:74, its blank symbol is 0, and its tape
is two-way infinite. Its SHA-256 is
d99fe0163acc608b1bed679e9c72a941bd05d37c285e6db0a12cb111eca25e7b.

The original 3,397-site numerical proof-enumeration source is unchanged,
byte for byte. Its SHA-256 is
222bb33545c28307c2a358760ed9857f6897d43ef1bae010919a0abca7e2b8f4.

This is not a claim that d:39 and either target state have identical
behavior on every possible tape. Its two incoming contexts acquire
different interpretations. The proof works at complete arithmetic-loop
boundaries and supplies the necessary entry conditions from an inductive
source invariant.

2. The smallest example of the rotated transfer

At each affected ordinary loop, the old source is

    h:     decrement R3; zero -> exit, positive -> h+1
    h+1:   increment R4
    h+2:   jump to h

At genuine entry to each of these 30 loops, R4 is zero. Consider R3=3.
The new implementation follows these register values:

    entry h              (R3,R4) = (3,0)
    after first decrement          (2,0)
    after deferred increment       (2,0)
    after first positive tail      (1,1)
    after second positive tail     (0,2)
    after final zero tail          (0,3), exit

The old CI4 address now defers its increment. At the old jump address,
the new tail increments R4 and then tests R3. A positive R3 is decremented
and returns to the same tail address. Zero R3 exits after the final
increment. The initial decrement and final increment balance exactly.

For input R3=0, the ordinary entry-zero branch exits without entering the
new tail. This separate case is essential: entering a tail with donor zero
does perform one increment, whereas entering the entire loop with donor
zero performs none.

3. Frames and universal domains

A canonical frame has state d:0 and consists of a leading guard, the binary
source address, a zero gap, the register header, nine unary register fields,
and zero spare fields. Register order on tape is

    R2, R8, R1, R4, R3, R7, R0, R5, R6.

The register value r is represented by r+2 consecutive ones in its field.
All nine register values are natural numbers. The base address-to-header
gap is at least 49, and there are at least 24 zero spare fields. Eleven
independent natural exponents represent the nine register values and the
unbounded extensions of the gap and spare region.

A visit to d:0 alone is not a source boundary. The replay checks the entire
tape frame, the source address, the control, and the exact head position.
Internal visits to d:0 are allowed during a certified phase.

Let w=1 if the loop exit wraps to source PC0, and w=0 otherwise. A wrap
moves the frame origin left by one and increases the gap by one. The
positive decrement adds one to the gap. An increment adds one zero spare
field. All registers not mentioned below are preserved.

The five phase contracts at each of the 30 literal loop locations are:

  Entry zero, h:
      R3=0, R4=m -> exit, R3=0, R4=m;
      gap += w, spares unchanged, frame origin -= w.

  Entry positive, h:
      R3=N>0, R4=0 -> h+1, R3=N-1, R4=0;
      gap += 1, spares unchanged, frame origin unchanged.

  Defer, h+1:
      R3=d, R4=0 -> h+2, same registers and resources.

  Tail positive, h+2:
      R3=d>0, R4=m -> h+2, R3=d-1, R4=m+1;
      gap += 1, spares += 1, frame origin unchanged.

  Tail zero, h+2:
      R3=0, R4=m -> exit, R3=0, R4=m+1;
      gap += w, spares += 1, frame origin -= w.

Their common guarded assumptions are R1>0 and R7=0. The defer and positive
entry additionally require R4=0. The tail contracts allow arbitrary
independent destination and donor values; they do not assume an unstated
relation between the other registers and their sum.

The 30 loop heads are:
281, 427, 453, 558, 636, 792, 1018, 1167, 1439, 1517, 1543,
1648, 1778, 1934, 2141, 2219, 2341, 2367, 2498, 2569, 2595,
2674, 2726, 2829, 2906, 2958, 3089, 3220, 3374, 3394.

All five contracts are checked separately at every address, including the
last loop's wrapped exit. No address-generic decoder behavior is assumed.

4. Natural-number induction for the complete rotated loop

Write n for the initial R3 value. If n=0, entry-zero gives the complete
source effect immediately. Suppose n>0. The positive entry and defer give
the first tail frame with

    R3 = n-1, R4 = 0, gap = g+1, spares = s.

For every k with 0 <= k <= n-1, induction gives the tail frame

    R3 = n-1-k,
    R4 = k,
    gap = g+1+k,
    spares = s+k.

For k<n-1, R3 is positive and the tail-positive contract gives the next
instance. R1>0 and R7=0 are preserved. The gap and spare counts never
decrease, so every phase premise continues to hold.

At k=n-1, tail-zero performs the final increment and exits. Therefore

    R3_out = 0,
    R4_out = n,
    gap_out = g+n+w,
    spares_out = s+n,
    origin_out = origin_in-w.

These are exactly the original complete-loop effects on the admitted
entry domain. The old loop terminates because each positive iteration
decreases R3. The new loop terminates because its positive tail does the
same, followed by one final zero tail. Each physical phase has a finite,
nonnegative linear clock in its natural parameters. Their composition is
finite for every natural n. No uniform finite bound on n is used.

5. The exceptional borrowed increment

The remaining literal R3-to-R4 transfer loop is at PC3334. Its source
neighborhood is

    3333: CI4 -> 3334
    3334: CD3; positive -> 3335, zero -> 3337
    3335: CI4 -> 3336
    3336: jump -> 3334.

The new physical contract at 3335 preserves every register and resource
and jumps to 3333. Thus the already-existing increment at 3333 performs the
increment that previously occurred at 3335. PC3336 is unreachable in this
new phase graph.

At each positive iteration the resulting arithmetic sequence remains one
CD3 and one CI4. For initial values R3=n and R4=m at 3334, it exits3337
with R3=0 and R4=m+n; gap grows by n and spares by n. This argument allows
arbitrary m. No zero-destination condition is needed for this exception.
If entry is instead at 3333, its one leading CI4 is preserved as well.

No exterior source edge enters the interior of any of these 31 loops.
The independent source checker reconstructs that fact from the literal
binary-address graph. The borrow changes only its own interior control
and does not introduce an entrance to another loop.

6. Why the guards hold on every actual call

The proof uses a source invariant at true outer entries, rather than
conflating those entries with internal iteration heads. The source's 791
disjoint closed decrement/increment loops are replaced temporarily by
their exact complete register updates. This is an analysis graph; the
machine's source bytes and addresses remain unchanged.

The invariant uses numeric classes {0}, {1}, {2}, {3}, {4}, and {5,6,...}.
The last class is unbounded. It is initialized at PC0 with R1=1 and all
other registers zero. The independent checker reconstructs the binary
source tree and every edge, checks all closed-loop patterns and ingress,
then verifies initial inclusion and all 1,832 nonhalting abstract successor
images. It contains 1,565 reachable outer vertices.

At each of the 30 rotated loop entries, it proves R4=0, R1>0, and R7=0.
Consequently the guarded contracts cover every actual call. The invariant
does not need to hold at the internal tail, where R4 grows; the explicit
tail induction supplies those interior facts. The exceptional borrow is
proved for arbitrary R4.

7. Complete physical coverage and composition

There are 4,334 ordinary natural-input domains outside the replaced
intervals, 150 guarded rotated-loop phase domains, and one borrowed-jump
domain: 4,485 in total. Each ordinary CD instruction has both its positive
and zero domain. The source's unique halting ordinary domain is included.
All other ordinary registers are independently arbitrary naturals.

The independent action checker reconstructs the domain set and every
endpoint from the source. It checks every literal transition, every
word-rotation identity, and all 20 one-unit pump laws before using their
natural-number iteration. It records full head displacement and linear
clock expressions. A second path exports raw physical computation and
pump induction to the standalone Lean kernel, with independent endpoint
definitions and arbitrary exterior tape rays beyond explicit blank margins.

The exact all-blank startup reaches PC0 after 67,998 transitions, with
R1=1, every other register zero, frame head -4, gap 55, and 30 zero spare
fields. The boot check compares every marked tape cell with the claimed
canonical frame. The fresh kernel replay also checks the complete blank
run in contiguous, endpoint-matched segments.

Combining startup, ordinary contracts, the 30 guarded loop inductions, and
the exceptional borrowed increment simulates every finite source prefix.
No admitted phase can get stuck or halt early. Therefore the 182-state
table halts from blank tape exactly when the unchanged proof-enumeration
source halts, and hence exactly when the 183-state baseline halts.

The total whole-program composition is an explicit written argument
supported by kernel-checked physical domains and independent source
checks. This package does not assert a single end-to-end Lean theorem for
that composition.

9. Discovery versus admission

The successful discovery screen tested14,299 whole-arithmetic-body and
blank-start fixtures. It allowed the two incoming edges of a deleted
state to select different existing successors. This is how the two
distinct phase changes above were found together.

Those finite tests are search evidence only. The reduction's admission
rests on the unbounded phase replay, inductive source guards, complete
source-domain accounting, literal startup, and explicit loop induction.
The exact rebuild ensures that the certified table is the table obtained
by the two displayed changes and the deletion of d:39.
