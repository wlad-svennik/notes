# The consistency criterion for the Cantorian chart

CS means that every set equinumerous with its singleton image is strongly Cantorian. Equinumerosity and the singleton graph use set-coded Kuratowski pairs. This is the full axiom in *Choice and Consistency Strength in NF and NFU* by R. Gutin and E. Glazer, v15, §21.1, without a well-orderability hypothesis.

Write `O` for the original RPR chart of `Scope209.Upper` and `A₀` for bare NFU with Dedekind Infinity and full CS. The register code, binary simulator and interpretation of the chart are distinct parts of the argument. The precise source and table identifications are provided in the accompanying machine release. `NFCS.Machine201.a0_of_source_nonhalting` directly concludes `DirectNFU.HasA0Model` from nonhalting of the checked register computation shared by the 201-state and 199-state machines. The input is the warm register frame identified by its binary startup proof.

## From a chart to A₀

The nondegenerate chart has a model of FunctionNF and its usual interpretation of NFU. This interpretation has stratified characteristic abstraction and an internally nonfinite universe. Dedekind Infinity follows inside this model. The finite Frege cardinals form a set. Every representative of a finite cardinal omits some element of the universe, and adjoining such an element defines its successor cardinal independently of the choices. Taking away the new element shows successor is injective. Its image omits the empty cardinal. Both cardinal variables in the successor relation have the same type, so stratified comprehension collects its Kuratowski graph as a set. The old model package verifies exactly this argument in `DedekindNaturals.lean`, theorem `NFUCut.Model.naturals_dedekind`. Its `FNF.CountSet.to_a0_model` in `A0Models.lean` combines the internally coded proper self-injection with any verified derivation of full CS in that same FunctionNF model.

The compiled modules `RPRCurry`, `RPRBlend`, `RPRGraph` and `RPRCantorian` apply the chart law to every nonempty Cantorian set X. Its singleton image B is equinumerous with K[B]. Indeed, lift a bijection X ≃ B to its singleton image and compose with the homogeneous bijection {b} ↦ Kb. If h is a represented bijection B → K[B], unbox h to a permutation p on B of relative type −1 and write g for its inverse, of relative type +1. On B prescribe s(b) = K²(p²b), on K³[B] prescribe s(K³b) = K²(gb), and keep all other values in K²[B]. The two domains are disjoint because b is a singleton characteristic row, hence nonconstant. The new selector construction uses four nested characteristic abstractions and the two maps furnished by the earlier certificate `SingleImage.realizeUV`. Its off-domain values can vary within K²[B]. The forced equality `(w t)w = s t` yields the singleton graph on B directly by unboxing each side twice with a fixed read point. The finite-type graph formula is checked in `RPRGraph.lean`. `SingleImage.strong_down` transports the graph back to X. The empty X has its empty singleton graph. This gives full CS for each set of the interpreted model.

## From pure NF with CS to a chart

Use the canonical full function chart of a pure NF model. Its old K and B satisfy the constant and abstraction equations. One can take the normalized fixed-point tester with values KB and B. The stratified graph construction `TaggedFixedPoint.exists_tester` works for any two markers. To distinguish the two markers, if B = KB then Bx = B for all x. Inserting K²v and Kv as the first two arguments of the abstraction law forces B = v for every v, against nontriviality. The lemma `NFCS.Upper.normalized_full_chart` checks this construction in Lean and retains the precise old application and K.

For a represented selector s, let D be its range. Set R(a) = s(Ka) and P(a) = s((as)s). Both maps return to D. If RPR fixes each a in D, R is injective there because applying RP to R(a) recovers a. It is surjective because R(PR(a)) = a. Consequently the set of Kuratowski pairs ⟨{a},R(a)⟩ for a in D is a bijection from the singleton image of D onto D. Stratified comprehension collects these pairs since {a} and R(a) have the same type. By full CS the graph of a ↦ {a} on D is a set. Using that graph as a parameter makes x ↦ K(sx) representable in the canonical full chart. Its representative w obeys (wx)w = sx for every x. If some range element fails the probe, there exists w with sw equal to that element and the RPR implication has false antecedent for every x. The proof therefore uses no internal choice principle or cardinal comparison theorem.

`NFCS.Pure.CS` states the pure-membership semantic axiom with object-quantified Kuratowski graphs, bijectivity and the singleton operation. `NFCS.Pure.localLaw` proves the argument above. The normalized letter construction and `NFCS.Pure.chart_of_model` then supply the original application chart. `NFCS.Pure.source201_nonhalting_of_model` checks the resulting nonhalting statement for the register source shared by the 199-state and 201-state machines. In `NFCSFormula.lean`, `Formula.sentence` writes the full CS axiom in the pure membership language. `Formula.models_iff_CS` checks its semantic meaning. `Formula.source_nonhalting_of_consistent` applies first-order completeness and gives nonhalting directly from the syntactic consistency of NF together with this sentence.

For an implementation of the first-coordinate register source, `FirstCalibration.goal_iff_original` identifies the numerical derivability goal with that of the original RPR source. The argument therefore applies unchanged once its physical table has been verified.

The older exact 204-state joint-witness table also benefits from this observation. `Cantorian204.runs_forever_iff_joint_chart` and `Cantorian204.original_chart_of_runs_forever` cover its lower direction. In the upper direction `Cantorian204Native.runs_forever_of_normalized` applies to the normalized full chart just constructed. 

## The comparison used

In R. Gutin and E. Glazer, v15, Proposition 17.37 gives the choiceless A₀ comparison with ZFC plus the scheme asserting an n-Mahlo cardinal for every standard n. Theorem 21.1 gives the same consistency strength for pure NF with full CS. Transitivity gives Con(A₀) ⇒ Con(NF+CS), which closes the lower direction. The chart’s construction from pure NF+CS gives the upper direction. Both mathematical chart constructions are kernel-checked. `NFCS.Lower.a0_of_chart` takes the original chart and returns precisely `DirectNFU.HasA0Model`, and `NFCS.Lower.a0_of_nonderiv` starts directly from the original numerical nonderivability statement. Both report exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The remaining theory-strength identification uses the written equiconsistency comparisons in R. Gutin and E. Glazer, v15. The pure-NF upper endpoint includes its formal appeal to first-order completeness. This proof does not assume an internal proof of consistency in ZFC or in PA.
